Use this whenever you're working with a family's PPAS Integrated Service Delivery consent form: going through it with them, checking what a signed form allows, or handling a change. The form is easy to fill in wrongly and easy to misread, so this is for the whole team: supervisors, SLPs and OTs, and Parent Coaches.

In short

The form lets the PPAS partners, and any other providers the family names, share the child's information with each other. Families don't have to repeat their story, and services can be coordinated. It's valid for one year.

The three things that matter most for QuickStart

  1. QuickStart's minimum: the QuickStart column ticked in the first row with Diagnosis covered, the first “Use of information” box, and a signature. REVIEW
  2. In each column, tick ALL or ONLY items, never both. The ONLY list continues onto page 3.
  3. If anything is unclear, don't guess. Pause that sharing and ask the family what they meant.

The bigger picture

What the form is, and isn't

  • It is PPAS's Stage 2 consent, signed once a family accepts services. Stage 1 is CI&CC's intake and service-matching consent, valid for 90 days. MEMO
  • It is consent for the four PPAS partners (IWK Health, Nova Scotia Health, Hearing and Speech NS, and Autism NS's QuickStart), plus any other providers the family names, to collect, use and share the child's information with each other. FORM MOU §1.3
  • It isn't consent to take part in QuickStart. QuickStart's own forms cover that. MEMO
  • It isn't about how anyone contacts the family. It's about how the organizations share with each other. QS
  • It isn't a limit on record-keeping. After a year, sharing stops; nothing is deleted. MOU §16.1

Principles behind every answer here

  • The consent is the conversation. The form is the written record of it, so review it with the family before they sign. OP GUIDE §4
  • Families stay in control. They choose partner by partner and type by type, and can change their mind at any time. Changes aren't retroactive. MEMO FORM
  • Share only what's needed. MOU §8.2
  • Never read a form more broadly than the family clearly gave it. If it's unclear, pause that sharing and ask them. MEMO MOU §8.5

How it works for QuickStart

  • Signed in the supervisor's intro meeting through DocuSign, together with QuickStart's own two forms. It must be complete before Session 1. WIKI
  • Stored in Greenspace and ShareVision. In Greenspace it goes in a “Service Delivery Consent Form” note with Clinician(s) ticked. MEMO WIKI
  • QuickStart relies on it to receive the child's service-match and diagnosis information, to coordinate with the family's other providers (e.g., their HSNS SLP), and to send the Discharge Report back to CI&CC at the end. WIKI QS
  • When things change: adding someone needs a new signed form (pre-filled); removing someone needs only written notice. FORM MOU §11 QS REVIEW

This tool doesn't replace the PPAS form or the review conversation with the family. Nothing you tick here is saved or sent anywhere, and there are no fields for names.
